My story

I am a student developer and digital storyteller with a strong interest in accessible communication, user-centered design, and meaningful online experiences. My work focuses on helping organizations share their mission through websites that are clear, welcoming, and easy to navigate.

I enjoy creating work that combines structure and creativity, especially when it helps people connect with an idea or community. Whether I am building a landing page, refining a brand story, or designing a user experience, I care about making information easy to discover and enjoyable to use.
